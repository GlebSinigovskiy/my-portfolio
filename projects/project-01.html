ABOUT THE PROJECT

ROOMMATE is a furniture rental service for people with temporary lifestyles. The brand offers an easy way to furnish a space without committing to long-term ownership.

01 / DESIGN CONCEPT

A playful visual identity built around movement, flexibility, and everyday living. Bold typography, bright colors, and a simple character create a friendly brand that feels modern, energetic, and approachable.

02 / THE CHARACTER

The mascot represents the idea of moving furniture without the stress. A simple human figure carrying different pieces of furniture adds personality and turns the service into something more familiar and relatable.

03 / FLEXIBLE BY NATURE

ROOMMATE is designed for people who are always moving, changing, and trying something new. The identity reflects this temporary way of living through a flexible system of graphics, messages, and compositions.
