ABOUT THE PROJECT

BREW.OS is a coffee shop imagined as a digital operating system, blending café culture with the visual language of software and interfaces. The identity uses a blue-and-white palette, bitmap illustrations, soft gradients, and coffee-related slogans styled like lines of code, creating a playful, contemporary system-like aesthetic.
